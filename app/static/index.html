    How the maps are made
    • Images: Sentinel-2 L2A of the most recent summer (1 June to 15 September), chosen automatically: up to 6 images spread over the season (3 before and 3 from 1 August), each under 10% cloud, shadow or snow over the area, at least 5 days apart. With fewer than 4 such images, up to 4 of the most recent images under 1% cloud. The area must fit in one Sentinel-2 tile.
    • Fields: dense green crops can look like forest on early-summer images. Where the images disagree by more than 0.35 and at least two of them show under 0.3, the date with the lowest cover is used.
    • Model: the 5 final U-Nets, each predicting the 10th, 50th and 90th percentiles of canopy cover (share of the 10 m cell under canopy above 2 m), combined and calibrated.
    • Conservative: true cover below it in about 10% of cells. Optimistic: above it in about 10%. Central: the median.
    • Accuracy on 39 held-out Ontario sites: mean absolute error 0.085. Outside Ontario's forest, expect larger errors.
